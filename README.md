# Mohammed Amine TALHI — Portfolio

Personal portfolio of a Supply Chain Engineer (EMINES – Mohammed VI Polytechnic
University), specialising in planning, scheduling and industrial flow optimization —
from shop-floor data to decision.

**→ [mohammed-amine-talhi.github.io](https://mohammed-amine-talhi.github.io)**

---

## What's inside

**About** — introduction, portrait, and the soft skills behind the work.

**Experience** — five roles on an animated timeline: the final-year project at OCP
Group (MILP sequencing across the mine → washing plant → train-loading chain), an
engineering internship at LAMMOC in Rio de Janeiro, and student-council treasury.

**Education & industrial visits** — EMINES and the Baccalaureate, followed by six
French industrial sites visited with the school — Weldom, Rungis, ReFood, Stellantis,
Carrefour and FM Logistic — each with photos and the write-up.

**Projects** — thirteen projects, filterable by discipline, spanning optimization,
machine learning, full-stack development and robotics. Each can carry its report,
presentation deck or poster.

**Skills** — an icon-only toolbox across supply chain, data, development and design.
Selecting a skill reveals the projects that actually used it, and jumps to them.

**Leadership** — basketball, clubs and student life, each opening as an illustrated
article with its own photo gallery.

**Contact** — every channel, plus the CV in the language you are reading the site in.

---

## Details

Fully bilingual, French and English, with a switch in the navbar — every project,
role and description exists in both.

Dark theme throughout, built around a cursor-reactive WebGL background. Animation
intensity is configurable, and the whole site respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Content is synced from a private résumé manager, so the site and the CV never drift
apart.

---

## Built with

React · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Framer Motion · WebGL

Deployed automatically to GitHub Pages on every push.
